Batman is a superhero who appears in American comic books published by DC Comics. He was created by writer Bill Finger and artist Bob Kane and first appeared in Detective Comics #27 on March 30, 1939. In the DC Universe, Batman is the secret identity of Bruce Wayne, a wealthy philanthropist and businessman who lives in the fictional city of Gotham. After witnessing the murder of his parents, Thomas and Martha Wayne, as a child, Bruce vows to dedicate his life to fighting crime and protecting innocent people. Unlike many superheroes, Batman has no superpowers. Instead, he relies on his physical and mental training, detective skills, technology, and family wealth. He creates the Batman persona to strike fear into criminals and follows a strict rule against killing. Throughout his history, Batman has been joined by allies and members of the Bat-Family, including Robin, Batgirl, Alfred Pennyworth, and Commissioner James Gordon. He also faces many famous enemies, including the Penguin, the Riddler, Two-Face, and his greatest enemy, the Joker.
